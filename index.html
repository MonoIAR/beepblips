How it works

No magic / just math
  1. One event per syllable. Han characters speak one at a time, Latin words split at vowel groups, and numbers are read as words. Each syllable gets one hashed vowel, onset, and tone.
  2. Pitch and mouth are independent. Four salted hashes scatter pitch, vowel, consonant, and jitter, so the same text always sounds the same without sounding like five recycled sounds.
  3. Punctuation conducts. A question lifts the tail, an exclamation raises the clause, an ellipsis drops and stretches it, and a comma takes half a beat.
  4. Pinyin mode speaks Mandarin. Type ni3 hao3 or hǎo; initials, finals, and the four tones map to gestures, formants, and pitch contours.
  5. Two-stage API. analyze() produces a serializable score; render() turns it into samples. Keep the score and swap the voice.
  6. Deterministic. The same text, options, and seed produce byte-identical WAV data. The page makes no network requests.
